    Load the game's ROMs
    
        
    
          This emulates the original arcade board, so it runs a dump of the
          game's ROM chips, such as MAME's lagunar.zip for Laguna
          Racer. The files are checked and kept in this browser only.

    How to play
    
          
        
    
        
    
          Race against the clock and score as much as you can. Reach the score
          set by the operator's DIP switches to earn extended time. The
          accelerator sets your speed: low gear pulls hard out of the curves,
          high gear reaches top speed.
